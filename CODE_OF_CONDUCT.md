# Code of conduct

This is a small project about a video game. The rules are short.

## What's expected

- Argue about heroes, not about people.
- Assume the other person plays a different bracket, role or region than you, and that their experience is real.
- "You're wrong because X does Y" is fine. "You're wrong because you're bad" is not.
- English isn't everyone's first language. Read past the wording.

## What's not allowed

- Insults, slurs, or harassment of any kind, including in hero or item names.
- Personal attacks, doxxing, or posting someone's private information.
- Sexual content, and anything sexual involving minors, which is reported rather than moderated.
- Spam, ads, referral links, or scripts that farm activity.
- Deliberately false data submissions.

## Scope

This covers issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and the project's pages.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([flexeykinDEV](https://github.com/flexeykinDev)) handles reports. Content that breaks these rules gets edited or removed, and repeat offenders get blocked from the repo.

To report something, open an issue if it's public and harmless, or email booby1546@gmail.com if it isn't. Reports stay private.

Decisions here are the maintainer's, and they can change these rules.
